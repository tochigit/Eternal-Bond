# Product Vision

## Product Name

Eternal Bond

---

## Mission

Help people build stronger, healthier, and more meaningful relationships through shared experiences, memories, communication, challenges, and intelligent assistance.

---

## Vision

Become the world's leading platform for strengthening human connections.

Eternal Bond is not a dating app.

It is a relationship platform for:

- Friends
- Couples
- Families
- Long-distance relationships
- Best friends
- Siblings
- Parent and child
- Mentors

Anyone who wants to build a stronger connection.

---

## Core Values

- Meaningful over addictive
- Quality over quantity
- Privacy first
- Emotional intelligence
- Trust
- Long-term relationships

---

## Product Goals

- Help users stay connected.
- Encourage meaningful conversations.
- Create unforgettable memories.
- Celebrate milestones.
- Strengthen existing relationships.
- Preserve memories for years.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Become the default app people think of when they want to strengthen an existing relationship.
